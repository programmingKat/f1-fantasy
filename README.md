# Project: F1 Fantasy! ! 🏎️💨

### Goal: Create a full-stack Express app with authentication and CRUD functionality

## Result: An F1 fantasy app 

* Create your own team! 
* Select a chassis and your drivers, then submit to see your team!
* Change tires depending on race day conditions with the slider. 
* You can delete your team !


## How to Use

1. Check out the [F1 Fantasy](https://picky-v3j9.onrender.com/).
2. Create an account if it is your first time logging in!
3. Sign in once you sign up
4. Create your team by choosing the following
    * a chassis
    * driver team and driver #1 - drivers dynamically populate when you choose the team
    * driver team and driver #2 - drivers dynamically populate when you choose the team
  
5. Create your team
7. You can edit tires using the slider
8. You can delete your teams
